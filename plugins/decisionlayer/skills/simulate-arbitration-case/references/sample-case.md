# Sample case: the unreturned security deposit

A made-up dispute for trying the simulator. Every person, address and fact here is fictional. Pass the values below to `run_simulation` unchanged.

## question_for_arbitration

Must the landlord return the tenant's $2,400 security deposit, or was the landlord entitled to keep it for cleaning and repainting?

## plaintiff_name

Morgan Ellis

## respondent_name

Riley Tanaka

## financial_demand_usd

2400.00

## governing_contract

RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made on June 1, 2025 between Riley Tanaka ("Landlord") and Morgan Ellis ("Tenant") for the apartment at 48 Alder Court, Unit 3 (the "Premises").

1. Term. The term runs from June 1, 2025 to May 31, 2026.

2. Rent. Tenant shall pay $2,400 per month, due on the first day of each month.

3. Security Deposit. Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,400. Landlord shall return the deposit within 21 days after Tenant vacates the Premises, less any amounts needed to repair damage beyond ordinary wear and tear or to clean the Premises to the condition it was in at move-in. If Landlord keeps any part of the deposit, Landlord shall give Tenant an itemized written statement of deductions, with receipts or estimates, within the same 21 days.

4. Condition of Premises. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and shall return them in the condition received, ordinary wear and tear excepted. Tenant shall not paint or alter the Premises without Landlord's written consent.

5. Move-out Inspection. Either party may request a joint inspection in the final 14 days of the term.

6. Arbitration. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Lease, including any dispute over the security deposit, shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by DecisionLayer under its rules then in effect. The arbitrator's award is final.

Signed: Riley Tanaka, Landlord. Morgan Ellis, Tenant.

## plaintiff_argument

I moved out on May 31, 2026, the last day of the lease. Before leaving I had the apartment professionally cleaned on May 29 and paid $280 for it. I asked Riley for a joint move-out inspection by text on May 20, and Riley replied that there was no need.

It is now more than 21 days since I moved out. I have not received my $2,400 deposit, and I never received an itemized statement of deductions. Section 3 of the lease requires both within 21 days. On June 30 Riley told me by text that the deposit was being kept for "cleaning and repainting" but gave no amounts and no receipts.

The walls had only the ordinary scuffs of a year of living there. I did not paint or alter anything. Ordinary wear and tear is excluded by Sections 3 and 4. I ask for the full $2,400.

## respondent_argument

Morgan left the apartment in worse condition than it was received. The kitchen had grease on the walls behind the stove, and the living room wall had a large patch of mismatched paint where Morgan covered something up without my consent, which Section 4 forbids. I had to repaint the living room and kitchen before the next tenant could move in. That cost $1,650. I also paid $350 for an additional deep clean of the oven and bathroom.

I admit I did not send a written itemized statement within 21 days. I was travelling in June and told Morgan by text on June 30 why I was keeping the deposit. Morgan knew the reasons. The total cost to me was $2,000, so at most Morgan would be owed $400.

## plaintiff_rebuttal

I did not paint anything. The mismatched patch on the living room wall was there when I moved in; it appears in the photos I took on June 1, 2025. Riley has not produced a receipt for the repainting or the cleaning, and the lease requires receipts or estimates with the itemized statement. A text message sent 30 days after move-out with no figures is not the statement Section 3 requires.

## respondent_rebuttal

I can provide the painter's invoice if asked. The patch may have been there at move-in, but the grease on the kitchen walls was not, and kitchens are not repainted after every one-year tenancy as ordinary wear and tear. Missing the 21-day deadline while travelling should not cost me the real expenses I paid.
